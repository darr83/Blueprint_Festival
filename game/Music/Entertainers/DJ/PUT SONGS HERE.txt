DJ
A DJ with a small booth, one of the entertainers you can hire for the camping and food fields. Plays dance music.

Put the DJ's songs in this folder. Any number works; 3 to 6 is plenty.
Every DJ you hire plays from this same folder.

Name each file with a number and the song's title, like this:

    01 Song Title.mp3
    02 Another Song.mp3
    03 The Big Finish.mp3

The number is the order the songs are played in, then round again until the set ends.
The files can be .mp3, .ogg or .wav. With no songs they still play, in silence.
