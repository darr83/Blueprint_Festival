THE ENTERTAINERS' MUSIC

These are the songs for the entertainers you hire from the Staff tab and put in a camping field
or a food field: the guitarist, the pianist, the drummer and the DJ. Each has a folder here.

    Guitarist   Country and folk on an acoustic guitar
    Pianist     Pop songs on a painted street piano
    Drummer     Rock rhythms on a little drum kit
    DJ          Dance music from a small booth

Put each one's songs in their folder, named like this:

    01 Song Title.mp3
    02 Another Song.mp3

They play their songs in number order, round and round, for each 45-minute set you give them.
Like the acts on the stages, they play in time to the beat: the first time a song is played the
game works out its beat and saves it beside the song as "01 Song Title.mp3.beat".

After adding files, open the game in Godot once so it can bring them in, then run it.
